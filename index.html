Method

Step by step

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  2. While the pasta cooks, warm olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the cherry tomatoes, zucchini, and peas. Cook for 4–5 minutes until the vegetables soften and the tomatoes begin to burst.
  4. Add the spinach and basil, then toss in the drained pasta. Pour in a splash of the reserved pasta water to create a glossy sauce.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and chili flakes. Finish with parmesan and toss gently until everything is evenly coated.
  6. Serve immediately with extra herbs and a little more parmesan on top for a fresh, vibrant finish.
Notes

Tips for the best result

  • Use a little pasta water to help the sauce cling to the noodles without becoming heavy.
  • If you want extra protein, add white beans or grilled chicken for a heartier meal.
  • Fresh basil and lemon zest will make the dish feel brighter and more aromatic.
